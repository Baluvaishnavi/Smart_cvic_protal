01. Problem Statement

Modern Challenges in Municipal Civic Governance

Citizen Trust Gap
📭

Opaque Grievance Channels

  • • Citizens submit complaints into bureaucratic black holes.
  • • Zero real-time status updates prompt repetitive duplicate calls.
  • • No transparent Service Level Agreement (SLA) visibility.
Operational Delay
⏳

Manual Triage & Sorting

  • • Municipal staff manually review hundreds of free-text submissions daily.
  • • High-severity hazards (gas leaks, open drains) wait behind minor noise issues.
  • • Recurring infrastructure failure hotspots go unnoticed.
Governance Gap
📊

Fragmented Intelligence

  • • No centralized real-time dashboard for city commissioners.
  • • Inability to measure department-level SLA compliance accurately.
  • • Budget allocation relies on guesswork rather than incident density data.

05. Dynamic Scoring

Rule-Based Prioritization Engine

Mathematical Formula Priority Score = Base Severity + Aging Penalty + SLA Breach Penalty + Community Multiplier + Hotspot Bonus
Base Severity
  • • Gas / Structural: 45 pts
  • • Water & Drainage: 35 pts
  • • Road Potholes: 30 pts
  • • Streetlight: 25 pts
  • • Waste: 20 pts
Aging & SLA
  • • Aging: +2.0 pts/day
  • • Cap: up to +20 pts
  • • SLA Breach: +15 pts
  • • Prevents backlog burying
Community Upvotes
  • • Upvotes: +2.5 pts each
  • • Similar Reports: +5.0 pts
  • • Neighborhood scaling
Priority Tiers
  • • CRITICAL: 85–100 (12h)
  • • HIGH: 65–84 (24h)
  • • MEDIUM: 35–64 (48h)
  • • LOW: 0–34 (72h)
